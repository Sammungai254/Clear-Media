    Billboards

    At Clear Media, our billboard services are designed to place your brand exactly where it matters most. We focus on high-traffic, high-visibility areas to ensure your message captures attention, drives engagement, and delivers real results.

    • Access to a vast network of premium outdoor media locations in urban and commuter hotspots.
    • Data-driven site selection to maximize impressions and brand recall.
    • Ongoing performance monitoring with flexible placement strategies to optimize reach.

    Digital Screens

    Our strategically positioned digital screens offer dynamic, high-impact advertising in high-footfall and high-traffic environments—ensuring your brand stays front and center at all times.

    We combine real-time media monitoring with detailed analytics to evaluate placement performance, audience engagement, and campaign impact—empowering you to make informed, results-driven decisions and refine your strategy for maximum visibility.

    Street Furniture

    At Clear Media, we integrate custom-designed street furniture—such as branded benches, kiosks, and shelters—into your campaign strategy to transform everyday urban spaces into impactful brand experiences.

    Through expert consulting and execution, we ensure each element serves both functional and promotional purposes, creating lasting impressions where people live, commute, and engage daily.

    • Insightful competitive analysis to identify prime engagement zones.
    • Bespoke campaign strategies leveraging street-level visibility.
    • Continuous optimization to maintain relevance and maximize ROI.

    Mobile Signs

    Our mobile signage solutions are designed to bring your brand directly to your audience—wherever they are. With unmatched flexibility, mobile signs amplify visibility in high-traffic zones, events, and targeted routes.

    From strategic deployment planning to real-time tracking and performance analysis, we ensure your mobile campaigns are dynamic, cost-effective, and constantly optimized for maximum reach and impact.

    • Custom routing to target specific audiences and high-traffic areas.
    • Real-time adjustments based on traffic patterns and audience density.
    • Detailed campaign reporting and optimization insights.

    Street Pole Advertising

    Street pole advertising on the Nairobi Expressway gives brands consistent visibility to motorists, commuters, airport users, and business travellers along one of Nairobi's most important transport routes.

    Street pole media delivers proximity, repetition, and strong corridor presence, making it an effective support format for larger outdoor campaigns, digital screens, pillar branding, and corridor domination packages.

    • Prime audience of motorists, commuters, and airport users.
    • High frequency and repeated exposure along a strategic corridor.
    • Cost-effective impact in prime expressway locations.

    Toll Station Branding

    Toll station branding offers advertisers premium visibility at high-attention points along the Nairobi Expressway. Positioned where motorists slow down, stop, or pass through controlled access areas, this format provides strong dwell time and repeated exposure to commuters, airport users, business travellers, and urban motorists.

    Ideal for brand awareness, product launches, financial services, telecoms, automotive, FMCG, and lifestyle campaigns, toll station branding gives brands a powerful presence within one of Nairobi's most strategic transport corridors—creating memorable engagement in a modern, premium road environment.

    LED Billboard Advertising

    LED billboard advertising gives brands a bold, dynamic, and high-impact platform to capture attention in premium outdoor locations. With bright digital displays, motion graphics, multiple creative rotations, and day-and-night visibility, LED billboards are ideal for brands looking to stand out in busy urban environments.

    This format allows advertisers to run flexible campaigns, update messaging quickly, schedule creatives by time of day, and showcase multiple products or offers within one campaign cycle—especially effective for product launches, tactical promotions, events, real estate, and lifestyle campaigns.

    • Bright, dynamic displays with day-and-night visibility.
    • Flexible scheduling with multiple creative rotations.
    • Professionally managed sites for strong, premium presence.
